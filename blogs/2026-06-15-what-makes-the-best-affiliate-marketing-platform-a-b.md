Affiliate marketing has evolved into one of the most effective strategies for driving sales, acquiring new customers, and expanding brand reach in a highly competitive digital landscape. This buyer's guide argues that the success of an affiliate program depends not just on the strategy itself but largely on the platform used to manage, track, and optimize it.
