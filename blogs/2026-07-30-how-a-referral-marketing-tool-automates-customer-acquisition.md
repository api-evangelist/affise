Customer acquisition has become increasingly expensive across digital channels. Paid ads are more competitive, organic reach is harder to sustain, and users are more skeptical of traditional advertising. In this environment, word-of-mouth remains one of the most powerful growth drivers – but it doesn’t scale on its own.
